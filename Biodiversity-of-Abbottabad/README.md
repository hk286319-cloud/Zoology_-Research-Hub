🦋 Biodiversity of Abbottabad
1. Introduction
Biodiversity refers to the variety of living organisms present in an ecosystem. Abbottabad, located in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan, has diverse natural habitats that support different forms of animal and plant life.
2. Research Objectives
To explore animal biodiversity in Abbottabad.
To document different groups of animals.
To understand the ecological importance of biodiversity.
To highlight the importance of wildlife conservation.
3. Major Animal Groups
Mammals
Birds
Reptiles
Amphibians
Insects
4. Ecological Importance
Animals play important roles in maintaining ecosystem balance through pollination, seed dispersal, food chains, and population regulation.
5. Conservation
Protection of natural habitats.
Prevention of illegal hunting.
Awareness about wildlife conservation.
Protection of endangered species.
6. Conclusion
Biodiversity is essential for ecological stability. Documenting animal diversity can help improve scientific understanding and promote conservation awareness in Abbottabad.
